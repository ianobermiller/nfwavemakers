A debate stays in Your Debates after its ballot is submitted, so the same card shows in both Your Debates and Submitted Ballots.

- [ ] Leave a debate in Your Debates only until this judge submits a ballot
- [ ] Keep that debate in Submitted Ballots, with the winner
- [ ] Keep Judge a Round when the judge has no open assigned debates
